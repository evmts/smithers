A call that names its own time is not cut at that time: `Sandbox.callLimit` gives a call whose input names `timeoutMs` or `timeoutSeconds` that time and `callMs` more, for the work its flow does around the command, held to what the frame has left. A sandbox `run` with `timeoutSeconds: 600` whose job settled after its timeout had been answered as timed out at 600 s.
