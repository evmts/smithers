# Conversation timeline

A desktop rail beside the conversation maps recorded asks and answers to transcript anchors. Clicking jumps. Arrow keys, J/K and Home/End move and jump; Escape focuses the transcript entry. The visible band follows scrolling and resize, including in branch conversations. Narrow screens and conversation columns below 600 pixels hide the rail, so a branch side pane cannot overlap its code view. Rows keep a fixed height while labels arrive. `@smthrs/ui` supplies `ChatTimeline` and the public `ChatTranscript.rail` slot.

Settled nonempty asks and answers launch `timeline/summarize` on the browser durable engine, independently of answering, while the rail shows them: the conversation's window, which Load earlier widens, never every message the device holds. A device that opens a long conversation so starts at most a window's worth of runs; one per message it held kept its engine and its device store busy for minutes ([local-first conflicts](../work/local-first-conflicts.md)). At most two run at once, across the home and the branch conversation a tab follows, and an entry already waiting or running isn't queued again. A window's worth started at once kept the engine busy after every reload: on smithers.sh on 2026-10-22 it woke 60 cut-off runs together, and a branch page's own `branch/track` waited 7 s to start and 6 s to settle behind them, so its conversation showed 20 to 45 s after the page. `timeline/SummarizeEntry` calls `ModelStep` through `/api/model/summary`, with at most 8,000 input characters and 40 output tokens. The cheap `summary` role is an Admin's setting (`admin/SetModel`), else the Worker's `SUMMARY_MODEL`; with neither, the endpoint refuses the call `409 no_route`, the step fails `unconfigured`, never retried, and the tab starts no more summaries, in any conversation, until the app opens again: a load costs at most two refused calls. Streaming, failures and waits display honest state labels. The role uses the existing endpoint's permissions, reservations, metering and quotas.

Successful summaries persist on the device in TanStack DB. Full-content SHA-256 identities invalidate changed entries, and deterministic run ids replay sealed model results after reload. A stale result cannot overwrite a changed entry. Deleting a source message removes its persisted summary, including messages outside the shown window; window eviction alone keeps the summary. Summary dispatches are scoped independently, so a defect cannot stop later entries, and one stalled run leaves the other turn free. Each changed settled entry the window shows costs one model request at the configured tariff; unchanged cached entries cost zero. Cross-device shared summaries are not implemented. Provider acceptance before sealing retains ModelStep's documented at-least-once charge window.

`timeline.summary` logs/spans expose role, ready outcome and replay status; `smithers_timeline_summary_calls` counts summary attempts, including failed calls and rejected output. Provider policy refusals fail `refused`, outages fail `unavailable`, and absent configuration remains `unconfigured`. Projection failures log only `timeline.summary.unavailable` or `timeline.summary.defect`; a missing summary model logs `timeline.summary.unconfigured`. Deletions emit `timeline.summary.deleted`, a `timeline.summary.delete` span and `smithers_timeline_summary_removals`. `TIMELINE_TELEMETRY` keeps these records through the relay. No transcript text or summaries are logged. The engine journal retains model input/output as private run data rather than telemetry.

The compiled first-party contribution lives in `src/features/timeline`; its intended manifest declares `chat.rail`. Until decision 0017's new Manifest/PluginHost loader lands, chat explicitly mounts the contribution. There is no separate private data interface.
